What is the Five Forces model?The basics in brief: the model, the five forces and the bridge to the SWOTExpandCollapse

The Five Forces model (industry structure analysis) by Michael Porter is a tool of strategic management for analysing a company’s external environment and determining the attractiveness of an industry – because that is shaped by the market structure.

The five forces

At the centre is the rivalry within the industry. Four outer forces act on it: the bargaining power of suppliers, the bargaining power of buyers, the threat of new entrants and the threat of substitutes. The stronger these forces, the lower the profitability – and with it the attractiveness of the industry.

Link to the SWOT analysis

The results are ideal external inputs for the SWOT analysis: strong forces usually become threats there, protective factors (e.g. high entry barriers) become opportunities. In this way the SWOT builds directly on the Five Forces analysis.
